Arima

Definition. An ARIMA$(p, d, q)$ process is a discrete-time stochastic process $\{X_t\}$ such that the $d$-th difference $W_t = \Delta^d X_t = (1-B)^d X_t$ satisfies the ARMA$(p,q)$ equation

$$ \phi(B)\,W_t = \theta(B)\,\varepsilon_t, \qquad \varepsilon_t \overset{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2), $$

where $\phi(B) = 1 - \phi_1 B - \cdots - \phi_p B^p$ and $\theta(B) = 1 + \theta_1 B + \cdots + \theta_q B^q$ are the AR and MA polynomials in the backshift operator $B$, and $d \geq 0$ is the integration order.

Key result (Wold decomposition). If $\phi(z) \neq 0$ for all $|z| \leq 1$ (invertibility of AR part) and $\theta(z) \neq 0$ for $|z| \leq 1$ (invertibility of MA part), then the ARMA component $W_t$ admits the unique causal representation $W_t = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \psi_j \varepsilon_{t-j}$, where $\psi(B) = \phi(B)^{-1}\theta(B)$ and $\sum_j \psi_j^2 < \infty$. Differencing $d$ times maps a unit-root nonstationary process to a covariance-stationary one, underpinning the "I" component. The Ljung–Box statistic $Q(m) = n(n+2)\sum_{k=1}^m \hat{\rho}_k^2/(n-k)$ under $\chi^2_{m-p-q}$ provides a standard residual diagnostic.

Literature context. Box and Jenkins (1970) introduced ARIMA as a systematic identification–estimation–diagnostic cycle; Hamilton (1994, Time Series Analysis) provides the canonical treatment. Open questions include: optimal order selection under model misspecification (AIC vs. BIC in finite samples with structural breaks), and whether differencing versus fractional integration ($d \in (0,1)$, ARFIMA) better captures long-memory phenomena in macroeconomic and climate series (Granger & Joyeux, 1980; Hosking, 1981).

Suggested investigation. Select a nonstationary economic series (e.g., monthly CPI or equity price index). Apply the augmented Dickey–Fuller and KPSS tests to determine $d$, use ACF/PACF plots to hypothesize $(p,q)$, estimate via exact maximum likelihood, validate with Ljung–Box and recursive residuals, and compare one-step-ahead forecast RMSE against a seasonal SARIMA$(P,D,Q)_s$ baseline.

Report-writing note. A rigorous write-up should state the unit-root test statistics and critical values, report parameter estimates with standard errors and likelihood ratio tests for order reduction, present ACF plots of raw and differenced series alongside model residuals, and include a rolling-window out-of-sample forecast evaluation with confidence intervals.
