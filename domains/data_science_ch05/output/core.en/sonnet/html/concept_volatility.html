Volatility

Definition. Let $\{S_t\}_{t \geq 0}$ be the price process of a financial asset. The log-return over the interval $[t, t+\Delta t]$ is defined as

$$r_t = \ln\!\left(\frac{S_{t+\Delta t}}{S_t}\right).$$

The volatility $\sigma$ of the asset is the annualized standard deviation of its log-returns:

$$\sigma = \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(r_t)} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1}{\Delta t}},$$

where $\Delta t$ is expressed as a fraction of one year. When returns are sampled daily, $\Delta t = 1/252$ (the conventional number of trading days per year), so the annualization factor is $\sqrt{252}$. Volatility quantifies the dispersion of price movements and serves as the primary measure of risk in continuous-time finance.

Worked Example. Suppose five consecutive daily closing prices are $S = (100, 102, 99, 103, 101)$. Compute the four daily log-returns:

$$r_1 = \ln(102/100) \approx 0.0198,\quad r_2 = \ln(99/102) \approx -0.0299,$$ $$r_3 = \ln(103/99) \approx 0.0392,\quad r_4 = \ln(101/103) \approx -0.0194.$$

The sample mean is $\bar{r} \approx 0.0024$ and the sample variance is

$$s^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{4}(r_i - \bar{r})^2 \approx 0.000894.$$

The annualized volatility estimate is therefore $\hat{\sigma} = \sqrt{0.000894 \times 252} \approx 0.474$, or roughly $47\%$ per year.

Key Theorem (Itô's Lemma applied to GBM). Under the Black–Scholes model, the asset price satisfies the stochastic differential equation $dS_t = \mu S_t\,dt + \sigma S_t\,dW_t$, where $W_t$ is a standard Brownian motion. By Itô's Lemma, the log-price $X_t = \ln S_t$ follows

$$dX_t = \left(\mu - \frac{\sigma^2}{2}\right)dt + \sigma\,dW_t,$$

confirming that $\sigma$ is precisely the standard deviation of log-returns per unit time. The $\sigma^2/2$ correction (the Itô drift adjustment) distinguishes arithmetic from geometric growth and is essential in option pricing.

Lab Cell.

import sympy as sp
import math

prices = [100, 102, 99, 103, 101]
log_returns = [math.log(prices[i+1]/prices[i]) for i in range(len(prices)-1)]

n = len(log_returns)
mean_r = sum(log_returns) / n
variance = sum((r - mean_r)**2 for r in log_returns) / (n - 1)
sigma_annual = math.sqrt(variance * 252)

print(f"Log-returns : {[round(r,4) for r in log_returns]}")
print(f"Sample variance : {variance:.6f}")
print(f"Annualized volatility: {sigma_annual:.4f}")
