The Mystery of the Missing Cookies

Imagine you bake cookies every day for a week. Monday: 12 cookies disappear. Tuesday: 8. Wednesday: 15. Thursday: 7. Friday: 20. Something strange is happening — but what?

Noticing Patterns

Data analysis is simply the art of looking carefully at numbers to find the story hiding inside them. Those cookie counts are data — raw facts. But when you line them up and ask questions, magic happens. Which day had the most disappearing cookies? Which had the fewest? Is there a pattern?

You might notice cookies vanish most on days ending in "y" — well, that's every day! But maybe more disappear on Fridays. That's a real pattern worth investigating.

Finding the Story

Data analysis means collecting, organising, and questioning information to answer "What's really going on?" You turn a messy pile of numbers into clear answers.

Now you try

Ask five friends how many books they read last month. Write down the numbers. Which is biggest? Smallest? What's the most common answer? Congratulations — you just did data analysis!
