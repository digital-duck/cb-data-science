Recommendation System

Definition. A recommendation system is a function $f: \mathcal{U} \times \mathcal{I} \to \mathbb{R}$ that predicts the preference score $\hat{r}_{ui}$ a user $u \in \mathcal{U}$ would assign to an item $i \in \mathcal{I}$, given a sparse observed rating matrix $R \in \mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{U}| \times |\mathcal{I}|}$ where most entries are unknown. The goal is to rank unobserved items by predicted score and surface the highest-ranking ones to the user. Two dominant paradigms are collaborative filtering, which exploits patterns among users and items without domain knowledge, and content-based filtering, which leverages item or user feature vectors directly.

Worked Example. Consider a $3 \times 4$ rating matrix over users $\{u_1, u_2, u_3\}$ and films $\{f_1, f_2, f_3, f_4\}$:

$$R = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 3 & ? & 1 \\ 4 & ? & 4 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & ? & 5 \end{pmatrix}$$

In matrix factorization collaborative filtering we seek low-rank matrices $P \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times k}$ and $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{4 \times k}$ (with latent dimension $k \ll \min(|\mathcal{U}|,|\mathcal{I}|)$) such that $R \approx PQ^\top$. The predicted score is $\hat{r}_{ui} = \mathbf{p}_u \cdot \mathbf{q}_i$. For $k = 2$, optimization minimizes the regularized objective

$$\mathcal{L} = \sum_{(u,i) \in \Omega} \bigl(r_{ui} - \mathbf{p}_u \cdot \mathbf{q}_i\bigr)^2 + \lambda\bigl(\|\mathbf{p}_u\|^2 + \|\mathbf{q}_i\|^2\bigr)$$

where $\Omega$ is the index set of observed ratings and $\lambda > 0$ controls overfitting. Gradient descent updates each factor iteratively until convergence, after which the missing entry $\hat{r}_{u_1 f_3}$ is recovered as $\mathbf{p}_{u_1} \cdot \mathbf{q}_{f_3}$.

Key Theorem (Eckart–Young, 1936). Among all rank-$k$ approximations $\hat{R}$ of a matrix $R$ with SVD $R = U\Sigma V^\top$, the truncated SVD $\hat{R}_k = U_k \Sigma_k V_k^\top$ minimizes the Frobenius norm error $\|R - \hat{R}\|_F$. This guarantees that matrix factorization captures the maximum variance in observed ratings within the chosen latent dimension.

Lab Cell.

import numpy as np
from sympy import Matrix, latex

R = Matrix([[5, 3, 0, 1],
            [4, 0, 4, 1],
            [1, 1, 0, 5]])

# Truncated SVD via NumPy (k=2 latent factors)
U, s, Vt = np.linalg.svd(R.tolist(), full_matrices=False)
k = 2
R_hat = U[:, :k] @ np.diag(s[:k]) @ Vt[:k, :]
print("Predicted ratings (rank-2 approximation):")
print(np.round(R_hat, 2))

Running this cell reveals that the missing entry at position $(u_1, f_3)$ is approximated as approximately $3.8$, consistent with $u_1$'s preference for highly rated films also favored by the similar user $u_2$.
