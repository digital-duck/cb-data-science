Predictive Analytics

The Weather Guesser Game

Have you ever looked at a dark, cloudy sky and thought, "I should bring an umbrella today"? You didn't know it would rain — but you made a smart guess based on what you'd seen before. That's predictive analytics!

What's the Big Idea?

Predictive analytics means using patterns from the past to make smart guesses about the future. Your brain does this all the time. If you always feel tired after skipping breakfast, you predict tomorrow will be rough if you skip it again.

Computers do the same thing — but with millions of examples. A computer might study thousands of basketball games and notice: "Teams that lead by 20 points at halftime win 95% of the time." Now it can predict the winner before the game ends!

The Recipe

  • Collect past data (what happened before)
  • Find patterns (what tends to follow what)
  • Apply to new situations (make your prediction)
