Data Privacy

Definition. Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a dataset over a universe $\mathcal{X}^n$, and let $\mathcal{M}: \mathcal{X}^n \to \mathcal{Y}$ be a randomized mechanism. The mechanism $\mathcal{M}$ satisfies $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-differential privacy (DP) if, for every pair of adjacent datasets $D, D' \in \mathcal{X}^n$ differing in exactly one record and every measurable event $S \subseteq \mathcal{Y}$:

$$\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D) \in S] \leq e^{\varepsilon} \Pr[\mathcal{M}(D') \in S] + \delta.$$

The pure case $\delta = 0$ is denoted $\varepsilon$-DP. The parameter $\varepsilon$ quantifies privacy loss; smaller $\varepsilon$ implies stronger protection at the cost of greater noise injection.

Theorem (Gaussian Mechanism). Let $f: \mathcal{X}^n \to \mathbb{R}^k$ have $\ell_2$-sensitivity $\Delta_2 f = \sup_{D \sim D'} \|f(D) - f(D')\|_2$. The mechanism $\mathcal{M}(D) = f(D) + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 I_k)$ satisfies $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-DP for $\sigma \geq \frac{\Delta_2 f \sqrt{2\ln(1.25/\delta)}}{\varepsilon}$.

Proof sketch. The privacy loss random variable $\mathcal{L} = \ln \frac{p_D(y)}{p_{D'}(y)}$ is Gaussian with mean $\|\Delta\|_2^2 / \sigma^2$ and variance $4\|\Delta\|_2^2 / \sigma^2$; bounding $\Pr[\mathcal{L} > \varepsilon]$ via Mills' ratio yields the $\delta$ expression. $\square$

Literature context. The foundational DP framework of Dwork et al. (2006) has been extended substantially: Rényi DP (Mironov, 2017) tightens composition bounds via moment accountants; zero-concentrated DP (Bun & Steinke, 2016) unifies several variants. Open questions include the optimal privacy-utility trade-off under local DP, and whether DP guarantees survive distribution shift at deployment.

Suggested investigation. Implement the Gaussian mechanism for private mean estimation on a real tabular dataset (e.g., UCI Adult). Vary $\varepsilon \in \{0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0\}$ and $\delta = 10^{-5}$; track empirical $\ell_2$ error against the noise-free estimator. Compute the theoretical error bound from the Gaussian mechanism calibration and compare to observed error, checking whether the bound is tight.

Report-writing note. A write-up should formally state the adjacency relation used, derive the noise scale from first principles, present the empirical privacy-utility curve with error bars across multiple trials, and discuss the gap between theoretical upper bounds and observed utility loss — noting any regime where the bound becomes vacuous.
